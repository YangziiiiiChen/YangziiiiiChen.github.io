Hi, I'm Yangzi Chen. I'm a fourth-year PhD candidate in Psychology at the University of Toronto, where I work with Dr. Kaori Takehara-Nishiuchi.

My research uses mouse models to study associative memory, with a focus on how the entorhinal cortex interacts with the hippocampus and neocortex as a single network.

I grew up in Dangyang, a city in Hubei Province in central China. Prior to Toronto, I did my undergraduate degree in psychology at Yuanpei College, Peking University. I enjoy films, TV shows, and music. I love the Coen brothers and Jarmusch, and I miss the old Kanye.
